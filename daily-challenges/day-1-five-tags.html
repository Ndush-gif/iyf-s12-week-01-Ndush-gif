What is semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML is the practice of using HTML markup to reinforce the meaning or semantics of the content, rather than merely defining its visual presentation or look. It involves selecting tags based on their inherent purpose and the type of content they enclose, ensuring that the structure of the document clearly communicates its intent to browsers, search engines, and assistive technologies.
